1. WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES
The Software looks for video recordings in a folder you choose (by default
Videos\Radeon ReLive) and re-encodes them to the AV1 format (or HEVC on older
graphics cards) to make them smaller. Re-encoding is lossy: the new file is very close to the original
but is not a bit-for-bit copy.

Depending on the mode you pick in Settings:
  - Test mode makes compressed copies in a separate folder and never changes
    your original recordings. This is the default after installation.
  - Move mode replaces each recording with its compressed version and moves
    the original to a folder you choose.
  - Replace mode replaces each recording with its compressed version and
    PERMANENTLY DELETES THE ORIGINAL. Deleted originals cannot be recovered
    by the Software.
Before an original is removed or moved, the Software checks that the new file
plays for the same length, has video and audio, and is smaller. This check
lowers the risk of losing a recording but cannot rule it out.

2. YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
You are responsible for choosing the mode and folders, and for keeping

3. AUTOMATIC OPERATION
If you turn on automatic compression, the Software runs by itself when the PC
has been idle (no keyboard or mouse input) for the number of minutes you set,
and pauses when you return. It uses your graphics card's video encoder, which
uses power and produces heat while it runs. You can turn automatic compression
off at any time in the app or from its tray icon.

4. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and an AMD Radeon graphics card with a hardware
video encoder and a current driver: Radeon RX 7000 series or newer for AV1,
or Radeon RX 400 series or newer for HEVC. On other hardware the Software
installs but cannot compress anything.
